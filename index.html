About us

PT. Compotec International is a private-owned manufacturer of plastic and synthetic rubber components, founded in 1994.

With more than 30 years of experience in injection molding and mold making, we design, develop and produce components in commodity, engineering and high-performance plastics, as well as thermoplastic rubber.

As an ISO 9001:2015, GMP and Halal certified company, we continuously improve our quality management system.
